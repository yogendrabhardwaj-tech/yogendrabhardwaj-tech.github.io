## Context

A public-transportation operator with a fleet of **2,000+ assets** needed real-time visibility into asset health and maintenance — and a faster, more reliable way to get technicians to the right asset with the right parts.

## Data platform

- **Azure Data Factory pipelines** feeding **Power BI** dashboards for real-time asset health and maintenance visibility.
- An **IoT ingestion pipeline** connecting sensor feeds to Azure Data Factory and **Databricks** for **predictive-maintenance analytics**, enabling servicing before failures occurred.
- **Executive dashboards and operational reports** on fleet performance, maintenance KPIs and cost tracking, used by **2,000+ operations staff**.

## Service management & field support

- **Power Automate** flows integrated with **ServiceNow** and **Workday** to automate work-order creation, maintenance notifications and **field-technician dispatch**.
- **Auto-generated pre-visit checklists and parts lists**, improving first-time fix rates.

## Why it matters now

Clean asset data, reliable pipelines and well-defined service workflows are exactly what AI agents for maintenance and field service need to stand on.
