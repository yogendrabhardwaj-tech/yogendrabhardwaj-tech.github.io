## Context

A credit-union and banking client was replacing the core system behind **certificate-of-deposit management** and **loan processing**. The goal: fewer manual hand-offs, faster decisions for members, and a platform that met **PCI** requirements.

## Architecture

- **Dynamics 365 CE** as the core platform, with an **entity schema and data models** designed for deposits and lending.
- **API integrations with DocuSign, Zelle and Expression** for **straight-through processing** — documents signed, funds moved and records updated without re-keying.

## Delivery

Full-lifecycle delivery: planning, stakeholder management and reporting, risk management and the Agile delivery lifecycle, leading cross-functional teams of engineers, architects and business analysts.

## Outcome

The platform serves **5,000+ users** and cut loan-processing cycle time by **30–40%**, while meeting PCI requirements.
